Pillar 01

Stakeholder Management

Expansion succeeds when every constituency understands what they gain, what they control, and what they must approve. Map early. Engage before the SPA.

Pillar 02

Selection Process

Drawn from FCMB’s Africa shortlist work with KPMG and the Bayport entry path: filter countries first, then choose the entry vehicle.

Level I

Macro & market screen

GDP scale, financial depth, political stability, FX convertibility, language fit, and presence of peer African banks.

  • Top 20 country longlist
  • Exclude sanction / extreme FX risk markets
  • Prefer English / familiar legal systems where possible
Level II

Capability fit

Match market structure to FCMB strengths: SME finance, digital banking, agric/business corridors, UK–Africa remittance links.

  • Banking concentration & SME gap
  • Digital readiness
  • Payroll / consumer lending TAM
Level III

Governance & entry realism

Regulatory openness, foreign ownership rules, license upgrade path, willing sellers, and time-to-market.

  • BoG / CB openness
  • Local partner requirements
  • Acquisition vs greenfield feasibility

Greenfield vs brownfield

Toggle the path. The playbook defaults to brownfield when a clean franchise exists; greenfield when licensing is open and no fit target is available.

Acquire a mid-tier bank

Fastest full banking ops. Deposit base, branches, FX. Lower license friction. Best when valuation is fair and the book is clean.

Acquire a large MFB / S&L

Lower entry price, strong retail/payroll base, faster clearance. Plan a 12–24 month license upgrade for full bank capabilities.

Greenfield license

Full design control and culture fit. Multi-year capital, systems, and distribution build. Use when no franchise meets quality gates.

Pillar 03

Due Diligence Process

Quality benchmarks, minimum workstreams, and extended coverage. Score the deal. The gate decides whether capital leaves the Group.

Quality benchmarks

  • Franchise leadership or clear #1–#3 path in target segment
  • Model adjacency to Credit Direct / FCMB capabilities
  • De-risked cashflows (e.g. payroll deduction) or diversifiable credit
  • Capital adequacy headroom after purchase-price adjustments
  • Management retention possible; culture not hostile to change
  • Regulatory path to digital products within 12 months

Minimum diligence requirements

  • Financial & tax (3–5 years audited + management accounts)
  • Credit / IFRS 9 vs local regulator provisioning gap
  • Legal: title, licenses, material contracts, litigation
  • IT / cyber: core banking, DR, vendor lock-in
  • HR & key-person / profit-share arrangements
  • AML / KYC / sanctions & consumer protection
  • Regulatory findings log and remediation cost

Why this playbook exists

Expand with a system — not a slide

African banking rewards institutions that treat cross-border growth as an operating discipline. This document is FCMB Group’s decision system for choosing where to play, who must be aligned, what must be true before capital commits, and how a newly acquired franchise joins the Group ecosystem.

The problem this solves

Deal excitement is not a strategy

Pan-African expansion fails in predictable ways: countries chosen for vanity maps, regulators surprised in the press, diligence that never forces a reprice, and integrations that leave the subsidiary orphaned from Group risk, funding, technology, and culture. Peers who lasted — and peers who retreated — teach the same lesson. Breadth without a playbook creates subscale subsidiaries; discipline creates franchises.

FCMB Group already has the ingredients of a regional retail engine: a listed banking parent, UK corridor capabilities, and proven consumer-lending craft inside the Group. What this playbook adds is the sequence — so every IC paper, LOI, and day-one plan answers the same questions in the same order.

What “good” looks like

Four decisions, in order

Read the playbook as a gate sequence. Do not open diligence on a market that failed selection. Do not sign an SPA while stakeholder legitimacy is unresolved. Do not celebrate close until the integration architecture into FCMB Group is staffed and funded.

01 Selection Criteria

Which countries and which entry vehicles earn our capital and management attention.

 02 Stakeholder Management

How regulators, owners, advisors, locals, and vendors are brought into a durable coalition.

 03 Due Diligence

What must be proven — and what kills or reprices the deal — before Group capital moves.

 04 Integration & Transformation

How a newly purchased business is absorbed into the FCMB ecosystem without losing the franchise.

How to use it

A working document for IC, deal teams, and country leads

Before LOI

Run Selection Criteria end-to-end

Do this. Clear the country cascade and the acquire-vs-build rule. Use the Peer Benchmark to pressure-test whether your entry logic matches how durable expanders actually grew.

Trap. Starting with a favourite target and reverse-engineering the market case.

Between LOI and SPA

Lock stakeholders and diligence gates together

Do this. Run Stakeholder Management and Due Diligence as parallel tracks with one IC narrative. Hard stops in diligence must show up in price, indemnity, or walk.

Trap. Treating regulator engagement as a closing checklist and credit truth as a post-deal problem.

From signing to day 100

Integrate into FCMB Group — then transform

Do this. Follow Integration & Transformation: control first, connect the subsidiary to Group standards and capabilities, then scale the value thesis.

Trap. Declaring “synergies” while the entity still runs on seller IT, local exceptions, and no Group reporting spine.
